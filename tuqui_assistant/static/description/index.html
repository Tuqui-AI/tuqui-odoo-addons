It sees the screen you are on

On a form, a list or a kanban, the panel knows the record, the filters and what you selected. No need to explain which one you mean.

On a form: it compares the open quote with the customer's past orders.

On a list: it groups the records you are looking at and answers with a table.

Nothing is saved without you

Ask for a change and it lands on the form as an unsaved edit. Save or Discard is still your click. Chatter replies open as drafts in the native composer.

The two new discounts wait on the form until you click Save or Discard.

It shows you where to click

Ask how to do something: a cursor walks the menu to the right screen, a green drop marks the next control, and the assistant waits while you do the step. It only navigates; it never types into a field or saves.

It opened Pricelists from the menu, marks New and waits for your click.
